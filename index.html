03 / process

No mystery. Four steps, in plain words.

  1. 01 listen

    We learn how you work

    A conversation about your business, the people you serve and what is getting in your way.

  2. 02 shape

    You see it before we build it

    Designs you can click through and a written scope with one clear price. Nothing starts until you approve.

  3. 03 build

    We make it, in the open

    We build in stages and show you each one as it is ready, so there are no surprises at the end.

  4. 04 look after

    We stay around

    Training for your team, help on launch day, and support for as long as you want it.
